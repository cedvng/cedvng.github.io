Who we are

Helping young people discover what they can become.

CEDV complements classroom learning through educational programmes, workshops, seminars, mentorship initiatives, competitions, awareness campaigns and other developmental activities.

Through interactive learning, student-focused engagements, leadership development and community outreach, we aim to promote intellectual growth, positive values, creativity, informed decision-making and active participation in society.

  • Student-focused learning
  • Confidence and life skills
  • Leadership and mentorship
  • Community participation
Our direction

Purpose with a long-term vision.

Our mission guides the work we do today. Our vision keeps us focused on the future we want to help create.

01 / Our mission

Our purpose in action

To enlighten, educate, inspire and empower school pupils and students through engaging educational programmes, workshops, seminars, mentorship, competitions, awareness campaigns and other developmental initiatives that promote knowledge, confidence, essential life skills, leadership, creativity and responsible citizenship.

02 / Our vision

A generation ready to make a difference.

To build a generation of knowledgeable, confident, responsible and empowered children and young people who realise their potential, pursue their aspirations and contribute meaningfully to the development of their communities and society.

What guides us

Values that shape every initiative.

These principles guide how CEDV works with learners, schools, partners and communities.

📚

Education and Knowledge

Promoting continuous learning, intellectual development and access to useful knowledge.

🌱

Empowerment and Inclusion

Our areas of focus

More ways to learn, grow and lead.

CEDV's programme directions are designed to respond to learners' interests, development needs and opportunities in their communities.

01 / LEARN

Educational Enlightenment

Seminars, awareness programmes and educational talks.

02 / DEVELOP

Skills and Personal Development

Practical workshops, life-skills sessions and confidence-building activities.

03 / LEAD

Leadership and Mentorship

Leadership summits, mentorship programmes and career guidance.

04 / THINK

Intellectual Development

Quizzes, debates, essay competitions and academic contests.

05 / ADVOCATE

Child and Youth Advocacy

Girl-child empowerment, education awareness and student-focused campaigns.

06 / CELEBRATE

Educational Celebrations

Children's Day, International Day of the Girl Child and other relevant observances.

07 / CONNECT

Community Engagement

School visits, outreach programmes and community education initiatives.

08 / RECOGNISE

Recognition and Encouragement

Awards, certificates and recognition of learners' achievements.

These are proposed and developing programme areas. They describe CEDV's intended direction and do not imply that every activity has already been implemented.
